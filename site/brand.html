Visual Language

Structure is the decoration.

A# avoids colorful SaaS conventions. Product screenshots, code, terminal output, rules, typography, and whitespace become the visual material.

Scale

Large editorial statements against small technical metadata.

Inversion

White-on-black and black-on-white sections create rhythm.

Rules

Thin precise dividers organize information without visual noise.

Whitespace

Generous empty space signals confidence and improves scanning.

Geometry

Angular, exact layouts subtly echo the A# mark.

Product-as-Art

CLI output, file trees, routes, and errors are primary brand visuals.

Incorrect Usage

The restraint is intentional.

The framework simplifies and structures web primitives; the brand does the same visually.

  • Do not recolor the mark.
  • Do not stretch or rotate it.
  • Do not add gradients, shadows, or 3D effects.
  • Do not detach or recreate the hash.
  • Do not place the mark on visually noisy backgrounds.
